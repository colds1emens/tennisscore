Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Club Flow ("we", "the app") is a team-management app for racquets clubs — tennis, pickleball, platform tennis and padel. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data — name, email, and (optionally) phone number: to create your account, link you to your team roster, and deliver notifications. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive only what Apple shares (you may hide your email).
  • Team activity — availability responses, lineup assignments, match results, and confirmations: the core function of the app.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not access your location, camera, contacts or photos.
  • The current version of the app contains no purchases of any kind.

Visibility inside your team

Availability, final lineups, and match results are visible to your team. Private captain notes, reliability metrics, and internal analytics are visible only to authorized captains, pros, and directors — never to other players.

Data retention, deletion & your rights

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Delete account) — this removes your account and unlinks you from team rosters. You may also request export or deletion by emailing mfc.daniil@gmail.com — honored within 30 days.

Children

Club Flow is intended for adults. A junior module, if introduced, will add guardian-managed accounts with additional protections.
