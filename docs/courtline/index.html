Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

CourtLine ("we", "the app") is a team-management and club-store app for racquets clubs. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data — name, email, and (optionally) phone number: to create your account, link you to your team roster, and deliver notifications.
  • Team activity — availability responses, lineup assignments, match results, and confirmations: the core function of the app.
  • Purchases — if you buy a gift card or club experience, we store the order, balance, and redemption history. Payment card data never touches our servers — payments are processed by Stripe; we receive only a payment reference.
  • Location (optional) — used only to show clubs near you, only while using the app, and only with your permission.
  • Camera (optional) — used only by club staff to scan gift-card QR codes at the front desk.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect precise location in the background.

Visibility inside your club

Availability, final lineups, and match results are visible to your team. Private captain notes, reliability metrics, and internal analytics are visible only to authorized captains, pros, and directors — never to other players.

Data retention & your rights

Your data is retained while your account is active. Request export or deletion any time at mfc.daniil@gmail.com — honored within 30 days.

Children

CourtLine is intended for adults. A junior module, if introduced, will add guardian-managed accounts with additional protections.
