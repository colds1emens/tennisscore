Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2026

Winner 105 (“the app”) is a free tennis scoreboard and practice-scoring app for iPhone and iPad. In plain language: the app does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics. It works entirely offline and never sends information off your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no servers and makes no network connections of any kind. Specifically, Winner 105 does not:

  • require or create an account, login, or profile;
  • ask for your name, email, phone number, or any contact information;
  • collect your location;
  • access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health, or calendars;
  • use any advertising or device identifier for tracking;
  • include any analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry;
  • include any third-party SDKs, trackers, or social frameworks;
  • show advertisements or offer in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

To be useful, the app saves some data locally on your device only — it never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Match and game history — scores, results, and the player or team names you type in;
  • Custom rule presets — point values and rules you save;
  • App settings — your chosen court theme and preferences.

This is stored with Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData). There is no cloud sync and no backup to our servers. Delete the saved history in the app, or delete the app, to remove this data.

Sharing a result

You can share a match result as an image, but only when you choose to. The standard iOS share sheet opens and you decide where it goes. The app does not upload the image anywhere itself.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising or external links. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. As the app collects no data, we don’t expect material changes.

Support

What Winner 105 does

A free, offline scoreboard for tennis with two modes:

  • Match — full classic scoring: 0/15/30/40 with deuce and advantage (or a no-ad deciding point), sets to 6 (win by 2), a 7-point tiebreak at 6–6, an optional 10-point super tiebreak, and best-of-3 or best-of-5. Tracks serve, prompts change-ends, shows break/set/match point. Tap either half of the screen to score; swipe down to undo.
  • “105” — a coach-run practice game scored by shot type (error, winner, volley, lob, smash). Every point value is adjustable with saveable presets. 1v1 or 2v2, custom target score, optional win-by-2, full undo/redo, a recent-events ticker, and a “who feeds the ball” indicator.

Four court themes, light & dark mode, animations, haptics, and the screen stays awake during play. Dynamic Type and VoiceOver supported.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data synced or uploaded? No. Everything stays on your device; the app is fully offline.

How do I undo a point? Tap the “Undo” button in the top bar, or swipe down on the scoring screen. Undo works any number of times.

How do I change point values in “105”? Open Settings → Points in “105” and adjust each value, or save a preset.

Is it really free? Yes — no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases.
