## Description (≤4000)
Tennis Score keeps the score so you can keep playing. Two modes cover real matches and coached practice, and everything works the moment you open the app.

No account. No sign-in. No ads. No in-app purchases. Fully offline - the app never makes a single network call, and every match, game and preset stays on your iPhone.

MATCH MODE
Full classic scoring, done right:
- Points 0/15/30/40 with deuce and advantage
- Optional no-ad deciding point
- Sets to 6, win by 2, with a 7-point tiebreak at 6:6
- Optional 10-point super tiebreak in place of a final set
- Best of 3 or best of 5
- Automatic serve tracking and change-ends prompts
- Pulsing BREAK, SET and MATCH POINT badges
- Coin toss to pick the first server

Scoring is one tap: tap your side of the screen to win the point, swipe down to undo. A set grid sits up top, the giant game score fills the center.

105 PRACTICE MODE
A coach-run drill game scored by shot type, no serve required:
- Error +1, Winner +5, Volley +10, Lob +10, Smash +20
- Every point value is fully customizable
- Save and switch your own rule presets
- 1v1 or 2v2 with player and team names
- Play to any target score (105 by default), optional win by 2
- Big capsule buttons per side, full undo and redo
- A ticker of the last 5 events
- A clear "who feeds the ball next" indicator

MADE TO LOOK GOOD
- Four court themes: grass, clay, blue hard court and a cool blue
- Light and dark appearance
- Rolling number score animation, confetti on a set, fireworks on victory
- Haptics that scale with the point value
- The screen stays awake while you play

SHARE THE RESULT
Turn any finished match into a result card and share it as an image - only when you choose to.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Data Not Collected. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, no crash reporting. Storage is local only. Nothing ever leaves your device.

ACCESSIBILITY
Dynamic Type and VoiceOver labels on every control.

Free. Offline. Yours.
