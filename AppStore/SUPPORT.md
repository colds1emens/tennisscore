# Tennis Score — Support

## What Tennis Score does

Tennis Score is a free, offline scoreboard for tennis on iPhone. There are two modes:

- **Classic match** — full tennis scoring with 0/15/30/40 game points, deuce and advantage (or optional deciding point), sets to 6 (win by 2), a 7‑point tiebreak at 6–6, an optional 10‑point super tiebreak in place of a final set, and best‑of‑3 or best‑of‑5 formats. The app tracks serve automatically, prompts you to change ends, and shows BREAK / SET / MATCH POINT badges. Tap either half of the screen to score that player; swipe down to undo. A coin toss picks the first server.
- **"105" practice game** — a coach‑run scoring game where points are awarded by shot type (for example Error, Winner, Volley, Lob, Smash). Every point value is adjustable, and you can save and switch between rule presets. Supports 1v1 or 2v2 with custom player/team names, a target score (105 by default, adjustable), optional win‑by‑2, full undo/redo, a ticker of recent events, and a "who feeds the ball" indicator.

The app also includes four court themes, light and dark mode, score and victory animations, haptics, and keeps the screen awake during play. It supports Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my data synced or backed up to the cloud?**
No. Everything — match history, custom rule presets, and settings — is stored only on your iPhone. The app is fully offline, makes no network calls, and has no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app removes all of its locally stored data.

**How do I undo a point?**
In a classic match, swipe down on the score screen to undo the last point. In the "105" game, use the undo/redo controls, which keep a full history of the session so you can step back and forward through points.

**How do I change the point values?**
In the "105" game's settings you can edit every point value (Error, Winner, Volley, Lob, Smash, and so on). You can also save your changes as a named rule preset and switch between presets later.

**How do I pick who serves first in a match?**
The app runs a coin toss at the start of a classic match to choose the first server.

**Does the app cost anything or show ads?**
No. Tennis Score is completely free, with no advertising, no in‑app purchases, and no subscriptions.

**Do I need an account to use it?**
No. There is no sign‑in or account. The app opens straight to the home screen.

**How do I share a result?**
After a match or game finishes, you can share the result card as an image using the standard iOS share sheet. Sharing only happens when you choose to do it.
