# Privacy Policy for Tennis Score

**Effective date:** [INSERT DATE, e.g. June 14, 2026]

Tennis Score ("the app," "we," "us") is a free tennis scoreboard and practice‑scoring app for iPhone. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles your information.

The short version: **Tennis Score does not collect any data about you.** There is no account, no sign‑in, no advertising, and no analytics. The app works entirely offline and never sends information off your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** We do not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store any personal data on our servers — because the app has no servers and makes no network connections of any kind.

Specifically, Tennis Score does **not**:

- Require or create an account, login, or profile.
- Ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information.
- Collect or use your location.
- Access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health data, or calendars.
- Use any advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier for tracking.
- Include any analytics, crash‑reporting, or telemetry tools.
- Include any third‑party SDKs, trackers, or social‑media frameworks.
- Show advertisements.
- Offer in‑app purchases or subscriptions.

## Information stored on your device

To make the app useful, Tennis Score saves some information **locally on your iPhone only**. This information never leaves your device, is never uploaded, and is not accessible to us or to anyone else:

- **Match and game history** — the scores, results, and the player or team names you type in for matches and for the "105" practice game.
- **Custom rule presets** — the point values and scoring rules you customize and save.
- **App settings** — your chosen court theme and other in‑app preferences.

This data is stored using Apple's on‑device storage (SwiftData and the system settings store). It stays on your iPhone, and there is no cloud sync, no backup to our servers, and no iCloud account linkage performed by the app.

You can remove this data at any time by deleting the saved history within the app, or by deleting the app from your iPhone, which removes all of its locally stored data.

## Sharing a result

The app lets you share a match result as an **image**, but only when you choose to do so. When you tap to share, iOS's standard share sheet opens and you decide where the image goes (for example Messages, Mail, or Photos). Tennis Score does not upload the image anywhere itself, and we never see it. Anything you do after the share sheet opens is governed by the privacy policy of the app or service you send it to.

## Children's privacy

Tennis Score does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for all ages and contains no data collection, advertising, or external links.

## Network access

Tennis Score is fully offline. It makes **zero network calls** and requires no internet connection to function.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and revise the effective date above. Because the app collects no data, we do not expect material changes.
